# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who want something personal made from a name: their own, or the name of someone close to them. They arrive curious, type a name, watch it grow into a flower, then keep it or give it to that person. They need no account to try it, and no knowledge of 3D, code or crypto.

## Product Purpose

Petal Pixel turns any name into a one-of-a-kind 3D flower that grows from a bud on screen. A visitor can keep the flower in their garden and share it. Success is a visitor typing a name within seconds, seeing it bloom, and saving or sending it.

## Positioning

The same name always grows the same flower. The flower is computed from the name itself, not picked from a library, so it belongs to that name and cannot be copied onto a different one.

## Operating Context

The first touch is usually a shared link or the home page on a phone or laptop. Growing a flower happens in the browser on the visitor's own device. Saving, the garden, the profile and exports live in the console behind sign-in.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Nine flower families, a bud-to-bloom growth view, a garden of saved flowers, PNG and GIF export, and public share pages.
- The promise on the public site is that a name grows into a flower. NFT ownership and printed lamps are not the headline.
- Minting exists in the code but is shown as "soon" on the public site. It needs a real network, a funded wallet and public file storage first.
- Printed flower lamps are a paused idea, not a product. Do not promise them.
- One name maps to one flower. "One name, one NFT" is still only a working assumption.
- The app runs at `petal.bexultan.dev`. `bexultan.dev` itself is the owner's personal portfolio and is a separate site.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Petal Pixel. Line: "a name becomes a flower". Interface language: English.
- Visual reference chosen by the owner, as a mix of two sites: from e-leora.com a calm light ground and a living 3D object shown like an exhibit, with the studio on the page; from thinkingods.com very large type and black pill buttons.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real: the working generator, the nine families, and the growth view.
- Absent: users, customers, testimonials, press, usage numbers, prices. Never invent these.

## Product Principles

- Let people try first. Growing a flower never needs an account.
- The flower is the proof. Show it working before explaining it.
- A name is personal, so treat every flower as a keepsake, not as a generated asset.
- Say only what is true today. What is coming is labelled as coming.
